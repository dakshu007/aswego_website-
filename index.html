06 About

A motorsport photographer in Kerala, shooting as we go

As We Go started trackside, with mud on the lens and a habit of standing too close to the berm. Today it is a motorsport and lifestyle photography studio that covers motocross and supercross racing, motorcycle launches, rider portraits and brand events.

We don't stage the story. We ride along with it, reading the light, the line and the people, and we deliver frames and reels that feel like you were there.

  • Based in Kerala, India
  • Travels across India
  • Stills + reels

07 FAQ

Good to know

What does As We Go photograph?

As We Go is a motorsport and lifestyle photography studio. We shoot motocross and supercross racing, motorcycle launches and rides, rider portraits, events and brand campaigns, plus everyday-life stories in between.

Where is As We Go based?

We are based in Kerala, India, and travel across South India and the rest of India for race weekends, launches and brand shoots.

How do I book a shoot with As We Go?

Send a DM on Instagram (@as.we.go__) or use the booking form on this page with your date, location and the kind of shoot you need. We reply with availability and a plan.

Do you shoot reels and video as well as photos?

Yes. Most shoots include vertical reels and short edits made for Instagram alongside the still photographs.

Do you cover full race events like supercross rounds?

Yes. We cover full race days, including practice, heats, finals, podiums and the paddock, for riders, teams, organisers and sponsors.

Can brands hire As We Go for motorcycle launches?

Yes. We photograph showroom launches, product reveals and ride-outs for motorcycle brands and dealers, and deliver images ready for social, web and print.
